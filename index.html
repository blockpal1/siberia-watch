Second pneumonia report (status)
Answered, not independently cleared. WHO asked Russia about a purported second case. DON620 (Oct 10) records Russia’s statement that there were no further cases of severe pneumonia linked to this event, and Rospotrebnadzor’s Oct 8 denial of second-illness social-media reports still stands. The DON does not independently verify a second institute-employee illness. Not counted as a case or death.
Generation

Death date
Outlets split Oct 1 vs the night of Oct 2. WHO DON uses Oct 2.
Diagnosis conflict
WHO DON620 (10 Oct) titles the event pneumonia of undetermined etiology. Russia’s NFP told WHO the fatality was not plague, the worker was vaccinated against high-threat pathogens worked with, two investigations found no biosafety incident, and more than 5,500 tests found no high-threat pathogens among contacts. WHO has not identified evidence of ongoing transmission on that information, but says the key uncertainty is the absent confirmed diagnosis, that it cannot independently judge whether contact monitoring was long enough, and that a laboratory-associated exposure cannot be fully excluded. Earlier plague and broken-tube media remain in conflict with the official pneumonia line. This page counts 1 death under contested etiology.
Exposure
Earlier reporting described a lab-linked exposure around Sep 25, 2026, at the Irkutsk Anti-Plague Institute. The index patient is the lab worker.
Contacts
Medical observation completed for all identified contacts (seven days per Russia→WHO). All remained asymptomatic. Contact screen in the DON: 2 COVID-19, 3 rhinovirus, 1 Haemophilus spp., other opportunistic infections — not plague. Baseline about 197. No confirmed secondary case.
