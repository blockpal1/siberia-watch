Suspected second death (unconfirmed)
WHO’s Abdirahman Mahamud (Geneva, Oct 8) said WHO verified a media report of a possible second pneumonia death in Irkutsk, deemed it credible, and has had no reply from Russia. Per CBC/Reuters, the report began in a Russian outlet, was picked up by the Daily Mail, and concerns a later pneumonia-like death at an Irkutsk hospital. Rospotrebnadzor called social-media reports of a second illness in a contact of the lab worker fake, and RIA says WHO “does not confirm” new cases. This page lists it as suspected and unconfirmed. It is not a case count and does not change the level.

Diagnosis conflict
No new official diagnosis. Rospotrebnadzor’s public line for the lab worker is still “pneumonia of unspecified etiology,” with no work-linked microorganisms and no lab accident (two biosafety commissions found none). On Oct 7 the agency added that the lab worker was vaccinated and had immunity to the dangerous infections she worked with. Cause of death remains officially unnamed; WHO’s Maria Van Kerkhove (Oct 7) said WHO still doesn’t know the cause of death and has asked about tests, materials, biosafety, and vaccination. The Kremlin spokesman said most media reporting on the case is false, which is a general dismissal, not a specific clearance. Plague reports remain in conflict with the official pneumonia line: Buryatia’s governor first said plague, then softened to “possibly” plague; earlier media described a lab-linked broken-tube account around Sep 25; Russia’s reply to WHO named no pathogen. This page counts 1 death under contested etiology. It does not treat the death as a cleared case or as a confirmed plague case count.
Exposure
Earlier reporting described a lab-linked exposure around Sep 25, 2026, at the Irkutsk Anti-Plague Institute. The index patient is the lab worker.
Contacts
Rospotrebnadzor (Oct 8) says medical observation has ended for all contacts (100%), up from more than 90% on Oct 7. Baseline about 197 (no new absolute headcount). The agency reports no infection-linked health problems and about 5,000 negative lab tests, says all preventive measures were completed Oct 8, and describes Irkutsk and Shelekhov as calm. No confirmed secondary case.
Geography
Unchanged ring: Shelekhov / Irkutsk. Neighbor border precautions remain in three countries: Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, and Uzbekistan. These are precautions, not export cases. No export or second-country case.
Official bulletins
WHO’s Director-General says WHO lacks a complete picture and can’t do a full risk assessment. WHO’s Oct 6 initial tiered assessment was moderate to low for Irkutsk, low for Russia, and very low for the WHO European region. CDC Director Erica Schwartz (Oct 7): risk to the US extremely low; CDC ready to screen travelers at JFK. Health Canada sees no risk. The US has sent Russia a formal diplomatic note, and the US Embassy in Moscow’s Oct 6 health alert repeats the existing Level 4 advisory rather than issuing a new one. No formal IHR notification, DON, or PHEIC step has been reported (last logged Oct 7).
Ground signal
No hit. An Irkutsk city Telegram channel loaded through about midday Irkutsk time on Oct 8 with nothing on hospitals, morgues, or funerals. The Shelekhov local news Telegram channel’s newest post is still dated 2026-10-02. VK was not checked.
Exit path
Not met. Contact observation is now 100% per the official line, but the second-case question is not cleared: WHO calls the possible second death report credible and Russia has not answered.
